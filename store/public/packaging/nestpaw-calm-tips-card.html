Help your dog settle

Simple habits for a quieter home

01

Keep a steady rhythm

Same meal, walk, and rest times each day help home feel predictable.

02

Make a quiet nest

A cozy corner with familiar bedding gives them a place to retreat.

03

Notice the calm moments

Softly praise quiet resting. Offer less fuss when they feel worried.

04

Move gently

A soft voice and slow movements help them feel safe.

05

Enrich the mind

Short walks, sniffing, and puzzle toys ease stress gently.
